## English

- **Name**: FRUIT STAR 3D
- **Subtitle**: Mechanical fruit reel machine
- **Promotional text**: Sit down at a real-feeling reel machine. Pull the lever, hit the stop buttons, line up the fruit. Offline play with virtual medals only.
- **Keywords**: slot,reel,fruit,pachislot,medal,retro,arcade,jackpot,cabinet,3D
- **Description**:

```
FRUIT STAR 3D recreates a Japanese-style mechanical fruit reel machine in 3D.

• Hands-on controls
Insert medals, press MAX BET or 1 BET, pull the lever and stop each of the three reels yourself. Buttons and the lever move, and the reels speed up, slip and stop with a physical model.

• STAR CHANCE lamp
Win a bonus and the STAR CHANCE lamp flashes on, sometimes the moment you pull the lever, sometimes after the reels stop. Aim for the 7s to start the BIG bonus.

• Three modes
Classic: play with your own medals; the balance is saved.
Free Play: medals refill automatically.
Practice: see the internal draw and practise your stop timing.

• Look inside
Mechanical view removes the shell to show the reel drums and hopper. Gallery view lets you turn the cabinet freely.

• Offline, no data collected
No internet connection, ads or in-app purchases. Your records stay on your device.

This app is a simulation for entertainment. Medals are virtual, cannot be exchanged for money or prizes, and no real money is wagered. No real machine's trademarks, artwork or odds are used.
```
